Privacy policy

Squiddler Dictionary does not collect, transmit, or sell any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, and no analytics or tracking of any kind.

The entire dictionary ships inside the app, so it works fully offline. Any words or definitions you add, and your settings, are stored only on your own device. They never leave it, and no one else can see them. Deleting the app removes them.

Because the app collects no data, there is nothing to request, export, or delete from any server.

Last updated: October 14, 2026

About the word list

The dictionary starts from the public-domain ENABLE word list plus newer and variant words from Open English WordNet (CC BY 4.0), for 192,586 words in all. Definitions come from Open English WordNet and the public-domain 1913 Webster's Dictionary, rewritten in modern English, with AI-written definitions for the rarest words.
